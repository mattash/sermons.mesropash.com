And of course, my number one all-time best sermon illustrator is my son Sion. And so I recently was having a struggle with him. We saw fireworks on the Fourth of July, and he said that he wanted to have fireworks, and I said, "That's a horrible idea." At first he wanted to just throw it and hear the sound, and then he wanted to throw them all at one time and hear the sound, and then he wanted to throw them at things. And so it keeps escalating. And I had to stop him, of course, 'cause that's what we do as parents, is we have to give structure to our kids' lives, a safe structure so they learn. But of course, he didn't wanna listen to me.
