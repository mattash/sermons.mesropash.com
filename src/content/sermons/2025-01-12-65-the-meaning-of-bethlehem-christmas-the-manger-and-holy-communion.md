[Concluding adult message]

To conclude our message for today, for our adults who are here, who perhaps learned something already from our children's sermon, I would like to connect the element of the story of the Nativity of Jesus Christ which has to do with the transience of Christ's holy parents, of the Holy Family, of Saint Mary and of Saint Joseph. When they had to leave their home to be registered in Bethlehem, and then shortly after, because of the pogroms of King Herod, they had to leave their homeland altogether and go to Egypt to keep their son, Jesus, safe. Their future was unclear, and they lived like homeless people for a time, not knowing what tomorrow would bring, until eventually they would be able to return to Nazareth to raise their son, Jesus Christ, in glory and in perfection so that he would be able to give his life so that we may be restored.

However, there was that time of uncertainty where they had to live by the hope of God. And so our prayer today is that for all of our brothers and sisters in Armenia, but also to those in Los Angeles who are living uncertain of their future and do not have a place that they can call home at this moment, we should only pray that a portion and lot of the hope of God is given unto them so that they should abide and survive until such time as they, too, are restored to the fullness of their lives.
